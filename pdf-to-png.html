Why choose PNG?

PNG uses lossless compression, which means it does not discard image detail in the same way as JPG. It can be a useful choice for PDF pages containing text, diagrams, charts, screenshots or line art. PNG files are often larger than comparable JPG files.

PNG or JPG: which should you use?

  • PNG: useful when retaining crisp text, diagrams and fine edges is important.
  • JPG: useful when smaller image files are more important and some compression is acceptable.
Tip: PNG output can take more storage space. Large PDFs may take longer to process, especially on phones or devices with limited memory.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert a multi-page PDF to PNG?

Yes. The converter processes the pages of the selected PDF and displays the resulting images for download.

Is PNG better than JPG for PDF text?

PNG can preserve sharp text and line edges because it uses lossless compression. JPG may create smaller files, but its compression can introduce artifacts around text.

Can I download PNG pages separately?

Yes. You can download an individual converted page or use Download All for the complete set.

Does the converter upload my PDF?
