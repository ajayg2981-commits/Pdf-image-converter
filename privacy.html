Information we process

The website may receive ordinary technical information from hosting/CDN services, such as IP address, browser type, device information and request information, according to those providers' policies.

Your files

PDF and image files selected in the converter are processed by JavaScript in your browser. We do not operate a backend conversion server for the basic converter.

The website loads open-source JavaScript libraries from CDN providers, including PDF.js and jsPDF. Your browser contacts those providers to retrieve the library files. Those providers may process technical request information under their own privacy policies.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The basic converter does not require an account. If analytics or advertising services are added in the future, they may use cookies or similar technologies. Any such services should be reviewed under their respective policies and applicable consent requirements.

Children's privacy

This website is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.
